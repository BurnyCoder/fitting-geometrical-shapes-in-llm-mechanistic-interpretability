## Known confounder

- **ringing curve c + sum_k a_k cos(k*pi*s) e_k**, which the fitter represents as `ringing`. When the similarity between points decays smoothly with an ordinal or latent variable (distance-decaying covariance, random walks, autocorrelated signals), PCA produces this curve, so an arch or helix seen in the top PCs is not evidence of a curved representation on its own. Sources: Novembre & Stephens 2008 (Nature Genetics); Diaconis, Goel & Holmes 2008; Antognini & Sohl-Dickstein 2018; Shinn 2023 (PNAS): <https://doi.org/10.1038/ng.139>, <https://arxiv.org/abs/0811.1477>, <https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.08805>, <https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2311420120>
