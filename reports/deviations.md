# Deviations from the preregistration and exploratory additions

Every change relative to [preregistration.md](preregistration.md) is listed here with its date and reason, as the preregistration requires. Analyses that were not preregistered are labelled **exploratory** here and everywhere else they appear.

| Date | Item | Preregistered | What was done | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-31 | Baseline sample size | B1, B2 and B3 run on the synthetic test set | B1–B3 run on a fixed subset: the first `baseline_per_cell` = 20 samples per family in each of the 12 cells, 3,120 samples. H1/H2 are paired on this subset. The method runs on the full test set. | B1 fits all 13 families with multi-start refinement (61 refinement runs per cloud). On the full 39k-sample set that would take more than 10 GPU hours on the 8 GB laptop GPU. |
| 2026-10-31 | Ablation budget | "3 seeds per ablation" | Each ablation is trained with a shorter, shared budget (12k steps / 60 min). It is compared against **A0**, the unchanged method retrained with the same short budget (configs `ablation_A*.yaml`). The number of seeds per ablation is reported with the results. | A matched-budget reference isolates the effect of each ablation from the effect of training length. The total GPU budget is limited. |
| 2026-10-31 | Training length | 100k steps | Steps are set from the measured speed (≈0.23 s/step at batch 512) and capped at 110 wall-clock minutes per seed. The steps actually completed are recorded in `results/<run>/train/summary.json`. | 8 GB laptop GPU budget. |
| 2026-10-31 | Counting-task text (R4) | Replication recipe text (FineWeb) | Text comes from `NeelNanda/pile-10k` documents, whitespace-normalised and wrapped with `textwrap.fill(width=150)`. | The corpus was already cached locally. The manifold is defined by line position, not corpus content. |
| 2026-10-31 | Bhalla et al. value ranges (R6–R8) | Templates from their Table 1 | The paper gives templates and sample sizes but not value ranges. We use: years 1801–1999 (n=199); 150 Fahrenheit values from −20 to 129; ages 1–99; 900 hex colours on an HLS grid (36 hues × 5 saturations × 5 lightnesses). The years template is "The date is year {Y}". | The paper does not specify the ranges. We chose ranges that match its sample sizes. |
| 2026-10-31 | Layer convention | "layer L" | `output_hidden_states[L]` in Hugging Face, i.e. the residual stream after L blocks. Models are truncated to L+1 blocks so index L is never the norm-applied final state. | The papers do not state an unambiguous index convention. |
| 2026-10-31 | **Exploratory** datasets | — | Added: R4b (Pythia-410m counting, layer 6), R5c ("It's {time} on {day}", Llama layer 19), R3 with n=20 features, class-mean versions of R5a/R5b (`*_means`), and controls `C3_grokking_WE_memorised` and `C3_tms_dense` (no preregistered label). | These are cheap extra views of the same phenomena. They are excluded from H3 and labelled exploratory. |
| 2026-10-31 | B2 classifier family fit | "B1's fit for the predicted family" | B2 reuses B1's per-family fits for the family it predicts. This is identical to running B1's fitter on that family, without recomputation. | Saves compute; the result is the same. |
